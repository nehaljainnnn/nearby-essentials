On OpenStreetMap, "near me" does not mean near you.

We tested it: press the locate button, search "pharmacy", and results appear outside India. Only when you type "pharmacy near Hosur Road" do you get local results. The search box finds place names. It does not search around you.

Location and search are disconnected

You must know to type the area name as well.

No distance in the results

You cannot compare which place is closest.

Travel time is a separate trip

Directions are a different tool, so you repeat the steps for each place.

How it works

  1. Set your starting point: use your location or type an address.
  2. Pick a category and radius: from 500 m to 25 km.
  3. Choose a place: read the distance and press Directions for travel times.

      Questions

      Why does "Use my location" not work?

      Open the site from its https link, allow Location in the browser lock icon, and make sure device location is on.

      Where does the data come from?

      OpenStreetMap contributors, through the Nominatim, Overpass and OSM routing services.

      Why is a place missing?

      OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers, so some places may not be mapped yet.
